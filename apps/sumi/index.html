About

Sumi: MyShopMate is your personal AI grocery assistant that helps you make smarter, healthier food choices, right in the store.

Point your camera at any grocery shelf and Sumi reads what's in front of you, finds the products that match your diet goals and flags anything that doesn't fit your needs or allergies. No more reading every label.

Ask Sumi about ingredients, nutrition or recipes. Set your allergies once. Save the picks you like to your ChopLog.
